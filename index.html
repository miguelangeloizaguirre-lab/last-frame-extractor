Frequently Asked Questions

How do I grab the exact last frame for AI tools like Runway or Kling?

Simply upload your video and click the "Last Frame" button. The app automatically seeks to the final millisecond and lets you download the exact image to use as a starting frame for video continuation.

Are my video files stored on any server?

No. All video decoding and image rendering are handled locally in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your privacy is 100% protected.
