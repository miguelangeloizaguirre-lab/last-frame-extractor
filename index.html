Frequently Asked Questions

How does LastFrameExtractor work?

Our tool loads your video file directly into your browser's memory, jumps straight to the final timestamp using HTML5 video APIs, and renders the last frame onto a high-resolution canvas for instant download.

Why is this useful for AI video generators?

Tools like Runway, Luma Dream Machine, Sora, and Kling allow you to continue or extend a video clip by uploading the ending frame as an initial image prompt. This tool gets you that exact frame instantly without taking screenshots.

Are my video files uploaded to a remote server?

No! All processing happens locally on your device. Your videos remain 100% private and secure on your local machine.
