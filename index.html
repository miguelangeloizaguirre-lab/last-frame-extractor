How to Use FrameSnap

FrameSnap is a high-precision video frame extractor designed for videographers, graphic designers, content creators, and researchers who need exact still photos from video files without losing image quality.

Key Features:

  • Single Frame Stepping: Advance or rewind video frame-by-frame (~0.033s increments for standard 30fps).
  • Lossless Snapshot Export: Save high-resolution frames in PNG or JPEG format matching full original video dimensions.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Videos are loaded locally in HTML5 memory. No network bandwidth consumed for file uploads.
  • Batch Zip Export: Save dozens of frame captures in one click.
